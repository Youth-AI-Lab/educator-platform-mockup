Understanding AI by experimenting

Why make AI a youth topic

Is an AI answer reliable? How does a machine create an image? What does AI change in our lives? AI can shape what young people see online and change the way they find information, learn or create. Games, hands-on activities and discussions help them understand how it works, check its answers and choose how to use it.

Even without experience of AI, facilitators and educators can rely on what they already know how to do: listen to young people, offer activities and lead discussions. They do not need to have all the answers. This topic touches on how young people find information, communicate and picture their future: helping them find their bearings is part of their educational role.

Understanding and using system prompts

A system prompt is a text of instructions given to an AI tool to guide its answers. It can set a role, a tone or rules to follow.

For whomThe facilitation team, before a workshop
Tools presentedClaude Projects, Gemini Gems

Understand

The same question, put to the same AI, does not get the same answer depending on the instructions it was given beforehand.

Try it

  1. Write

    Create a project or a Gem, and paste the system prompt below into it.

  2. Test

    Ask a question whose answer is known, then a question that cannot be answered.

  3. Adjust

    Change a single rule, ask the same questions again and compare.

You are an assistant helping a group explore a topic.

TONE
You answer in three sentences at most, in simple language.

RULES
You say "I don't know" rather than making up an answer.
You cite your source when you give a figure.

LIMITS
You give neither medical nor legal advice.
